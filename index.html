About简介

I am a Ph.D. student in Earth and Planetary Sciences at Harvard University, working with Prof. Stein B. Jacobsen. I received my B.S. and M.S. from China University of Geosciences (Beijing).

我是哈佛大学地球与行星科学系博士生，导师为 Stein B. Jacobsen 教授。本科与硕士毕业于中国地质大学（北京）。

Research研究方向

I

Planetary Science — How Earth, the Moon and Mars formed行星科学 —— 地球、月球与火星的形成

Combining isotope geochemistry with dynamical modeling to reconstruct how the terrestrial planets and the Moon were assembled, and what they were built from.结合同位素地球化学与动力学模拟，重建类地行星与月球的形成过程及其物质来源。

II

Critical Metal Deposits关键金属矿床

Studying critical metal deposits using isotopes and AI methods.用同位素与人工智能方法研究关键金属矿床。

Current project当前课题

Zn–Cu–Fe–Si isotopes of the Jinchuan Ni-Cu-PGE deposit金川 Ni-Cu-PGE 矿床的 Zn–Cu–Fe–Si 同位素

Using Zn, Cu, Fe and Si isotopes to understand the formation of the Jinchuan deposit.利用 Zn、Cu、Fe、Si 同位素研究金川矿床的形成。

Current project当前课题

Zircon Zr isotopes and the onset of plate tectonics锆石 Zr 同位素与板块构造的起始

Using Zr isotopes in zircon to constrain when plate tectonics began on Earth.利用锆石 Zr 同位素约束地球板块构造的起始时间。

Current project当前课题

Mg isotopes during mantle melting地幔熔融中的 Mg 同位素

Understanding how Mg isotopes fractionate during mantle melting.理解地幔熔融过程中 Mg 同位素如何分馏。
